Biochemistry Notes for MLT Students

Clinical biochemistry measures chemical constituents of blood and other specimens to support diagnosis, monitoring and screening.

Study note: This page is educational study material. Follow your institution's validated laboratory procedures for practical work.

Specimen and pre-analytical factors

Patient preparation, specimen type, collection technique, transport and storage can affect biochemical results. Hemolysis, lipemia and icterus may also interfere with some assays.

Glucose testing

Glucose can be measured in different specimen types and by different analytical methods. Interpretation depends on the test, collection conditions, reference interval and clinical context.

Enzymes and proteins

Laboratories measure enzymes and proteins such as aminotransferases, alkaline phosphatase, albumin and total protein. Results are interpreted alongside other laboratory and clinical findings.

Quality control

Internal quality control helps laboratories detect analytical problems before patient results are released. Calibration, control materials, documentation and corrective action are parts of a quality system.
