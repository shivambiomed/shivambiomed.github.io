Immunology Notes for MLT Students

Immunology studies the body's defense mechanisms and the laboratory detection of immune responses. It is important for understanding serology and diagnostic testing.

Study note: This page is educational study material. Follow your institution's validated laboratory procedures for practical work.

Innate and adaptive immunity

Innate immunity provides rapid, non-specific defenses, while adaptive immunity develops targeted responses involving lymphocytes and immunological memory.

Antigens and antibodies

Antigens can stimulate immune responses. Antibodies are immunoglobulins produced by B cells and plasma cells and can bind specific antigenic targets.

Serological testing

Serology may detect antibodies, antigens or other immune markers. Test interpretation depends on assay characteristics, timing and the clinical context.

Quality and controls

Positive and negative controls help assess whether a serological assay is performing as expected. Reagents, storage conditions and procedural timing should follow the validated method.
