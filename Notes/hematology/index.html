Hematology Notes for MLT Students

Hematology deals with blood cells, blood-forming tissues and laboratory investigation of blood disorders. These notes provide a foundation for MLT study and revision.

Study note: This page is educational study material. Follow your institution's validated laboratory procedures for practical work.

Blood cells

The major cellular components of blood are erythrocytes, leukocytes and platelets. Their number, morphology and function are assessed in routine hematology.

Complete blood count

A CBC commonly includes measurements related to hemoglobin, red-cell count and indices, white-cell count and platelet count. Results should be interpreted with the laboratory's reference intervals and clinical context.

Peripheral blood smear

A stained peripheral smear allows examination of red-cell morphology, white-cell morphology and platelet appearance. Smear findings complement automated analyzer results.

Coagulation

Basic coagulation testing includes assays such as prothrombin time and activated partial thromboplastin time. Correct specimen collection, citrate ratio and prompt processing are important for reliable results.
