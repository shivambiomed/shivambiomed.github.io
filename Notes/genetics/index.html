Genetics Notes for MLT Students

Genetics explains how biological information is stored, transmitted and expressed. MLT students encounter genetics in cytogenetics, molecular diagnostics and inherited disease testing.

Study note: This page is educational study material. Follow your institution's validated laboratory procedures for practical work.

Genes and chromosomes

Genes are functional units of hereditary information located within chromosomes. Human somatic cells normally contain 46 chromosomes arranged into 23 pairs.

Inheritance

Patterns of inheritance include autosomal dominant, autosomal recessive, X-linked and other mechanisms. Family history can provide important context for genetic investigations.

Mutations and variants

DNA changes can range from single-nucleotide changes to larger structural alterations. Their significance depends on evidence, gene function, population data and clinical context.

Molecular laboratory methods

PCR, electrophoresis, sequencing and other molecular methods can be used to detect or characterize nucleic-acid targets. Each method requires validated procedures and appropriate controls.
