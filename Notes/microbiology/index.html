Microbiology Notes for MLT Students

Microbiology is a core area of Medical Laboratory Technology. These notes introduce the laboratory principles used to examine microorganisms and support diagnostic testing.

Study note: This page is educational study material. Follow your institution's validated laboratory procedures for practical work.

Specimen collection

Good microbiology begins with the correct specimen, correct container, adequate volume, proper labeling and timely transport. The specimen should represent the suspected site of infection and be collected before antimicrobial therapy when clinically appropriate.

Microscopy and staining

Microscopy provides rapid information about cell morphology and arrangement. Differential stains such as Gram staining help classify bacteria by cell-envelope characteristics and guide further laboratory work.

Culture and identification

Culture uses suitable media and controlled conditions to recover organisms. Identification can combine colony characteristics, microscopy, biochemical reactions and validated laboratory methods.

Laboratory safety

Use appropriate personal protective equipment, hand hygiene, safe sharps handling, disinfection and biological-waste procedures. Follow the biosafety practices required by the laboratory.
